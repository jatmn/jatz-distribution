Quinityn sealed freighter

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: single vehicle design option for a Factorio mod, game asset concept.
Primary request: an original high-capacity freight hauler truck in the visual language of Yuoki Industries, informed by the Yuoki machinery, Engines, PFW truck factory, and YIR rail sprites inspected in this conversation.
Style: dense utilitarian retro-industrial 3D prerendered sprite aesthetic. Chunky cast metal housings, bolted segmented armor, deeply recessed machinery, black rubber tires, visible hydraulic cylinders and copper pipe bends, readable large components. Restrained wear at edges, soot around exhausts, slightly hand-painted game texture finish. Dark graphite steel with brushed silver mechanical edges and copper or brass fittings. Saturated painted body panels as described below. Circular radiator fans and ribbed engine covers. Bright accents occupy small functional areas.
Composition: ONE complete truck centered, isolated on a plain warm gray background, spacious margins, no scenery, no text or labels. Elevated orthographic three-quarter game view about 55 degrees above ground. Front points toward lower left, cargo body extends to upper right. Both roof and near side are clearly visible. Wheels physically attached under a continuous plausible chassis. Entire silhouette visible. Broad readable shapes at small game zoom. Light comes from upper left, short soft shadow to lower right, deep ambient occlusion. Truck fills about 80 percent of a landscape 3:2 image.
Reference to Factorio car: low sturdy exposed chassis, mechanically detailed wheels, visible engine parts, oblique overhead camera, clear paintable panels, unified lighting. This is a freight vehicle with a large obvious cargo volume. No weapons, no people, no rails, no hovering, no giant logos, no cinematic environment, no depth of field.
Design-specific brief:
A broad low eight-wheel advanced cargo hauler on four axles, all rigid one chassis. Short armored cab at the front with small recessed cyan windows. Huge enclosed weather-sealed cargo vault behind cab, comprising three connected boxy reinforced bays with beveled corners, thick charcoal-gray cast ribs, slate-blue metal doors and massive orange corner locks. The vault is for solid industrial freight and has obvious large rear loading doors. Small protected violet unicomp power cell behind cab, restrained violet illumination through two tiny circular inspection ports, copper tubing and silver heat exchanger fins. One large circular roof cooling assembly ringed in brass with dark radial fan blades. Quinityn purple accent and salvage machinery influence combined with PFW gray industrial frame. Strong muted steel silhouette with violet used sparingly. Practical wheels, deep tread, no hover or science fiction weapons.
