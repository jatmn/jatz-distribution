Armored freight trucks that keep your factory supplied by road. Build provider and request depots, lay road between them, and Quinityn freighters haul ten stacks of items or 750 fluid a trip from the nearest source by road, refueling themselves along the way. Inspired by Transport Drones and built for big factories, with buffers, priorities, circuit control and blueprints. Factorio 2.1, Space Age optional.
