Version: Unreleased
  Features:
    - Startup settings for the tank size of liquid provider depots and of liquid buffer depots, each 25,000 by default.
    - A delivering freighter's cargo bays show its load: light grey while empty, then the color of the item or fluid it picked up (water blue, copper orange, sulfur yellow...) on the way back. Cargo with no clear color keeps the usual slate blue.
    - In alt mode, loaded freighters show the icon of the item or fluid they carry. Empty freighters show none.
    - Freighters run over enemy units on the road, as trains do, dealing impact damage. They never harm players or other freighters, and never stop or turn aside for enemies.
    - Freighters out on deliveries make a little pollution, as Transport Drones' drones do.
    - A request depot whose freighter is destroyed asks construction robots for a replacement, as soon as its chest has a free stack for it. You can also put one in by hand.
    - A delivering freighter that is destroyed explodes and leaves a wreck, which disappears after 15 minutes, as the drivable freighter does.
    - Freighters need fuel. They come with a full tank, burn it as they drive, and fill up at fuel depots by themselves: one too low for where it is going stops at the fuel depot nearest by road first, then carries on. Any fuel item or burnable fluid works, from any mod.
    - Freighters too low on fuel to reach the provider stay home until there is fuel to get, never waiting at an empty fuel depot. The request depot's light is yellow while the fuel depots have no fuel and red when no fuel depot is connected by road; they set off as soon as one has fuel. Fuel in other depots stays ordinary cargo.
    - Hovering a freighter on the road shows its fuel (green, yellow when low, red when dry), and the request depot's window shows the fuel of the freighters in its slot.
    - A fuel item worth more than a freighter's tank (some mods' items hold 10 GJ) goes in whole, and the freighter keeps all of it until used up.
    - Petroleum gas burns for freighters, 50 to a full tank, unless another mod already gives it a fuel value.
    - Fuel depots take fuel items (by hand, inserters or robots) and burnable fluids (by pipe). Their window shows the fluid, with a button to flush it.
    - Map settings for a freighter's fuel tank (20 MJ by default) and how much it burns a tile (10 kJ by default; 0 turns fuel off).
    - The mod has a thumbnail, the freighter, shown in the in-game mod browser and on the mod portal.
    - Provider, request and buffer depots (item and liquid) have an Enable/disable circuit condition, like a logistic chest's: switched off, a depot sends no freighters and none come to fetch from it. The condition reads either the circuit network, leaving out the depot's own contents, or only the depot's own contents.
    - Road network monitor: built beside road, it sends what the provider and buffer depots on that road network hold to the circuit network, like a roboport. It has its own grey and bronze look, matching the liquid depots. Open it to choose what it reads: what provider and buffer depots hold, what provider depots hold, what request depots hold, or what request depots still need.
  Changes:
    - Mod setting tooltips are easier to read: each has icons, line breaks and colored text, with its range and default in green and yellow, and warnings in red.
    - Freighters can no longer be placed and driven like a car unless the new startup setting "Place freighters as cars" is turned on. Freighters already placed stay drivable.
    - Delivering freighters drive at about 4 tiles a second instead of 12, the default speed of Transport Drones' drones.
    - Every depot shows a circuit connector where red and green wires attach.
    - Freighters stop for 1 second at a depot's loading zone to load and to unload, instead of loading and unloading the moment they arrive.
    - Only belts, pipes and train tracks can be built on road. Depots and other buildings can't, though a depot's loading zone can be on road.
    - Request and liquid request depots with "Request from buffer depots" unticked now fall back to buffer depots for what no provider can supply, instead of ignoring them. Ticked, buffer depots still come first.
  Bugfixes:
    - A freighter killed by enemies is now lost, with its cargo. Before, it could go back into its depot's freighter slot as if its route had failed.
    - Freighters requested for a request depot whose chest has no free stack (it is full of cargo, say) are now brought once a stack frees up, together with any more asked for meanwhile. Before, the request was dropped.
  Compatibility:
    - With Mining Drones, Mining Drones 2 Remastered or Mining Drones Mod installed, mining depots are item provider depots of what their drones mine: freighters fetch it from the tile behind the depot, opposite its mining area, where the depot lays road like any other depot. They have a priority like provider depots.
    - Fuel depots placed in earlier versions are removed when the save loads: the fuel depot is now a chest with a tank under it.
    - Marked Transport Drones and its Factorio 2.1 forks (Transport Drones (Continuous), Transport Drones (Continued, 2.1, Unofficial) and Transport Drones By Yunli) as incompatible.
