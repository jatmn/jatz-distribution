Version: Unreleased
  Features:
    - Startup settings for the tank size of liquid provider depots and of liquid buffer depots, each 25,000 by default.
    - A delivering freighter's cargo bays show its load: light grey while empty, then the color of the item or fluid it picked up (water blue, copper orange, sulfur yellow...) on the way back. Cargo with no clear color keeps the usual slate blue.
    - In alt mode, loaded freighters show the icon of the item or fluid they carry. Empty freighters show none.
  Changes:
    - Delivering freighters drive at half their previous speed: 6 tiles a second instead of 12.
    - Freighters stop for 1 second at a depot's loading zone to load and to unload, instead of loading and unloading the moment they arrive.
    - Request and liquid request depots with "Request from buffer depots" unticked now fall back to buffer depots for what no provider can supply, instead of ignoring them. Ticked, buffer depots still come first.
  Compatibility:
    - Marked Transport Drones and its Factorio 2.1 forks (Transport Drones (Continuous), Transport Drones (Continued, 2.1, Unofficial) and Transport Drones By Yunli) as incompatible.
